Mr. President, Your Position is Strong

You command the world's greatest industrial economy and a continent unto yourself. The USA stands isolated by two oceans, rich, and unentangled—a position of extraordinary strength.

The Global Stage:

Europe: Germany rises aggressively under Hitler. France and Britain watch nervously.
Asia: Japan expands in Manchuria and China. The Republic of China fractures into warlord cliques.
Your Sphere: The Panama Canal anchors your influence. Gen. MacArthur commands your Pacific garrison in the Philippines.

What You Must Do Now:

Diplomacy First: Strengthen ties with Britain and France—they share your interest in checking German and Japanese expansion. Consider trade agreements and naval coordination.

Military Readiness: Your Philippines garrison is exposed. Begin fortifying island bases. Build naval strength—Japan eyes resource-rich territories.

Economic Policy: Invest in rearmament quietly. America's industrial might is your ace. The world will need American steel.

Stay Neutral (For Now): Avoid formal alliances that drag you into European conflicts you don't own. Profit from neutrality while preparing for the storm ahead.

Immediate Action: I beseech you to open trade negotiations with Britain and accelerate military modernization. Position yourself as the balancing power—neither conquered nor conquering, but indispensable.

What direction calls to you, Mr. President?
