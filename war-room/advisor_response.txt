Your Position: Isolated Strength

Current Reality:

Europe: Germany rearms aggressively; Britain and France watch nervously
Asia: Japan expands in China; the Soviet Union strengthens its Far East
Americas: You're the dominant power, unchallenged
Your Military: Small but professional; General MacArthur commands in the Philippines
My Counsel

Immediate Priority: Industrial Modernization ✓ Your Arizona initiative is sound. We must expand manufacturing across the Southwest and Midwest—the coming years will demand American steel and weapons.

Strategic Actions I Beseech:

Build your Pacific Fleet — Japan grows bold. Your Philippines garrison needs naval protection
Cultivate Britain as an ally — They'll need American support if Europe ignites
Maintain neutrality publicly — Americans oppose foreign entanglement; exploit this
Strengthen hemisphere ties — Latin America must remain in your sphere

Do NOT rush into conflict. Your advantage is patience and industrial preparation.
